Before you start

Does this event need a report?

Most events do. These are the exceptions for each classification. If you're not sure, report it and your Plant Manager will decide.

Safety

Not needed for HR-sensitive matters such as a fitness-for-duty violation, LOTO removal approved by the Plant Manager, or low-consequence clerical items.

Environmental

Not needed for administrative or clerical items of low consequence, at the Plant Manager's discretion.

Generation

Not needed for trips or run-backs with a known cause restored quickly, recurring issues already reported, or loss under 25% of one day's full output.

NERC

Not needed for administrative or clerical items of low consequence, at the Plant Manager's discretion.

Property damage

Not needed when the total estimated damage is less than $25,000.

Security

Reported at the Plant Manager's discretion. Some categories need immediate notice.

Outage delays and lost generation coming out of an outage only need a report if the Plant Director asks for one.
